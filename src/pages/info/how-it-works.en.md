# How it works

Each piece is split into lessons, one per voice: Soprano, Alto, Tenor and Bass. Pick your voice and follow your line on the score.

## The lessons, in order

1. **First listen.** Listen to the whole piece to get a sense of the ensemble.
2. **Slow reading.** Your part at a slower tempo, to read the notes and the text.
3. **In time.** Your part at the real tempo.
4. **Two voices.** Your voice together with one other voice, to check how the parts fit.
5. **With the choir.** The complete piece, with all the voices.
6. **Your turn.** Your voice is removed from the mix, and you sing it yourself.

## On the score

- The **cursor** moves along the score in time, so you always see where you are.
- Your **voice is highlighted**; the other voices are dimmed.
- **Lyrics and subtitles** follow the singing, line by line.

## Chapters

Videos are divided into chapters. Use them to jump straight to the part you need.
