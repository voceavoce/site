# Disclosure

Voce a Voce currently has no sponsored links.

If we add any in the future, they will always be marked "sponsored link" and will carry the attribute `rel="sponsored"`.

Free public domain sources always come first: the free source and edition are listed before any paid link. See [Sources and licences](/en/sources-and-licences/).

## Artificial intelligence

The notes on the pieces, the translations of the sung texts and part of the site copy are written with the help of artificial intelligence tools (Claude, by Anthropic) and edited by the people who run the project, who are responsible for them. Translations that have not yet been proofread are marked “not reviewed”. The original texts of the pieces are in the public domain and taken from the sources listed. If you find a mistake, write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).
