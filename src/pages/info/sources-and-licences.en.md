# Sources and licences

We use only **public domain** scores, that is works whose licence allows free use. The main sources are:

- [CPDL / ChoralWiki](https://www.cpdl.org): the Choral Public Domain Library.
- [IMSLP](https://imslp.org): a library of public domain scores.
- [OpenScore](https://musescore.com/openscore-lieder-corpus): a lieder corpus under the CC0 licence.

## Source and edition

For each piece we give the source and the edition. You will find them on the piece's page and in the video description.

## Corrected scores

When we correct a score, the corrected version is published on GitHub, in the [voceavoce/scores](https://github.com/voceavoce/scores) repository.

## Reporting an error

Did you notice a wrong note or word? Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/voceavoce/scores/issues) and include:

- the piece and the voice;
- the minute of the video where the error appears;
- what it should be instead, with the source if you know it.
